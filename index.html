About Me

Hi, I'm Md. Ridoy Hossain, a tech-driven entrepreneur, Robotics Engineer, and Mentor with over 7 years of industry expertise in Robotics Systems, Embedded Systems, Control Engineering, Autonomous Navigation, Python, and Mechatronic Integration.
